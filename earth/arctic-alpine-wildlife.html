01 — Overview

Arctic & Alpine, in
a nutshell.

Cold regions test every adaptation: thick fur, fat, hibernation and long migrations. Arctic tundra and high mountains are warming faster than much of the planet, putting specialist species under pressure.

02 — Adaptations

Built for
the cold.

Fur & fat

Dense fur over black skin and thick blubber keep polar bears warm in Arctic water.

Seasonal camouflage

Coats turn white in winter and brown in summer to blend in.

Epic migrations

Herds follow food across some of the longest overland migrations on Earth.

Mountain mastery

Big paws and long tails help them cross steep snow and rock.

Thin-air flight

These geese cross the Himalaya at extreme altitude, where air is very thin.

04 — Pressures

What is
changing.

01

Melting sea ice

Less ice means shorter hunting seasons for polar bears and seals.

02

Moving snowlines

Alpine species are pushed uphill until there is no higher ground left.

03

Thawing permafrost

Frozen ground is softening, changing tundra plants and releasing stored carbon.
