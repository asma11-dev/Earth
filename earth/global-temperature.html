01 — Overview

Global Temperature, in
a nutshell.

Earth has warmed by roughly 1.3°C since the late 1800s, mostly because we burn coal, oil and gas. The last ten years were the ten warmest on record, and 2024 was the first calendar year above 1.5°C.

02 — Causes

How the planet
heats up.

Natural, but thickened

Greenhouse effect

Gases like CO₂ and methane trap heat. That keeps Earth habitable, but we are making the blanket thicker.

Biggest source

Fossil fuels

Burning coal, oil and gas produces most of the greenhouse gases we add each year.

Forests & farming

Land use

Clearing forests releases carbon and removes trees that would absorb it.

Short but powerful

Methane

From cattle, landfills and gas leaks. Over 20 years it traps far more heat than CO₂.

Warming amplifies itself

Feedbacks

Melting ice exposes darker land and sea, which absorb more sunlight and warm further.

03 — Signals

Fingerprints of
a warming world.

Hotter years

Atmosphere

Global temperature records keep being broken, with the past decade the warmest ever measured.

Heat sponge

Oceans

The oceans absorb about 90% of the extra heat trapped by greenhouse gases.

Shrinking

Ice

Arctic sea ice in September has fallen by roughly 12% per decade since satellites began.

“The planet is running a fever, and we know the cause.”
