01 — Overview

Net Zero Pathways, in
a nutshell.

Net zero means cutting greenhouse gas emissions as far as possible and balancing the rest with removals. Clean power, electric transport, efficiency and protecting nature are already scaling quickly around the world.

02 — Levers

Six big
levers.

Solar · wind · hydro

Clean electricity

Replace coal and gas with renewables and storage.

Cars · heating · industry

Electrify everything

Electric vehicles and heat pumps run efficiently on clean power.

Do more with less

Efficiency

Better buildings, appliances and processes cut waste.

Hard-to-abate

Cleaner food & industry

New methods for steel, cement and farming cut the toughest emissions.

Forests · wetlands · soils

Protect nature

Stopping deforestation and restoring land keeps carbon locked up.

Last resort

Carbon removal

Technologies and nature-based removal cover what cannot be cut.

03 — Success stories

What is already
working.

Scaling up

Offshore wind

Near-100% renewables

Costa Rica
