01 — Overview

Extreme Weather, in
a nutshell.

Warmer air holds more moisture and energy. That is loading the dice towards stronger heatwaves, heavier downpours, longer wildfire seasons and more intense storms in many parts of the world.

02 — Hazards

Four ways
weather bites.

Silent killers

Heatwaves

Longer, hotter spells strain people, power grids and crops, especially in cities.

Heavier rain

Floods & storms

A warmer atmosphere releases more rain in a single event, overwhelming rivers and drains.

Dry and burning

Drought & wildfire

Hotter, drier conditions let fires start easily and spread quickly.

Stronger impacts

Tropical cyclones

Warm seas fuel storms that bring more rain and higher storm surge.

03 — Who is exposed

Most at
risk.

Heat islands

City residents

Concrete and few trees can make cities several degrees hotter than the countryside.

Livelihoods

Farmers

Drought, floods and heat can wipe out a season’s crops.

Storm surge

Coastal communities

Rising seas make every storm surge reach further inland.

“A hotter sky holds more water, more energy, more trouble.”
