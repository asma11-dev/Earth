01 — Overview

Antarctica, in
a nutshell.

Antarctica is the coldest, windiest and driest continent, almost entirely covered by ice. It has no permanent residents, is protected by the Antarctic Treaty, and holds most of the planet’s fresh water.

02 — Landscapes

Places that
define Antarctica.

Ross Ice Shelf

A floating sheet of ice about the size of France, edging the Ross Sea.

Antarctic Peninsula

The warmest part of the continent, with glaciers, penguins and whales.

The Dry Valleys

Rock-strewn valleys with almost no snow, among the driest places on Earth.

03 — Wildlife

Life on
the edge.

Near threatened

Emperor penguin

Breeding colonies are at risk as sea ice melts earlier each year.

Keystone species

Antarctic krill

Tiny crustaceans that feed whales, seals and penguins across the food web.

Endangered

Blue whale

The Antarctic subspecies was hunted close to extinction and is slowly recovering.
