01 — Overview

Europe, in
a nutshell.

Europe is compact but endlessly varied: 44 countries, glaciated Alps, Atlantic coasts, Mediterranean islands and Arctic fjords. After centuries of change, many regions are now rewilding forests, rivers and wetlands.

02 — Landscapes

Places that
define Europe.

The Alps

An arc of peaks across eight countries, source of the Rhine, Rhône and Danube headwaters.

Norwegian fjords

Deep glacier-carved inlets beneath northern lights and midnight sun.

Danube Delta

One of Europe’s best-preserved wetlands, a haven for pelicans and over 300 bird species.

03 — Wildlife

Life on
the edge.

Near threatened

European bison

Once extinct in the wild, bison now roam rewilded forests in Poland, Romania and beyond.

Vulnerable

Iberian lynx

Recovered from under 100 animals to well over 2,000 thanks to habitat restoration.

Vulnerable

Atlantic puffin

Seabird colonies are struggling with warming seas and fewer fish.

“Small continent, long memory.”

04 — Challenges

What is
at stake.

01

Fastest-warming continent

Europe is heating roughly twice as fast as the global average, bringing heatwaves and drought.

02

Farmland biodiversity

Intensive farming has reduced insects, birds and wildflowers across the countryside.

03

Shrinking glaciers

Alpine glaciers are retreating, changing rivers, tourism and water supply.
