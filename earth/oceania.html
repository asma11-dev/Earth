01 — Overview

Oceania, in
a nutshell.

Oceania stretches from Australia and New Zealand to thousands of Pacific islands. It holds the Great Barrier Reef, ancient deserts and rainforests, and wildlife found nowhere else on Earth.

02 — Landscapes

Places that
define Oceania.

Great Barrier Reef

The largest coral reef system on Earth, visible from space and home to thousands of species.

Milford Sound

A fiord in New Zealand with waterfalls dropping from steep, rainforest-draped cliffs.

Uluru

A sacred sandstone monolith in Australia’s red centre, central to Anangu culture.
