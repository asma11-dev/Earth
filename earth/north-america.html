01 — Overview

North America, in
a nutshell.

North America spans Arctic tundra, temperate forests, prairies, deserts and tropical coasts. It holds the Great Lakes, the Rocky Mountains and some of the planet’s most iconic national parks.

02 — Landscapes

Places that
define North America.

The Rocky Mountains

A spine of peaks stretching roughly 4,800 km from Canada to New Mexico.

Grand Canyon

Carved by the Colorado River, 446 km long and up to 1.8 km deep.

The Great Lakes

Five freshwater seas that shape weather, wildlife and cities across the continent.

03 — Wildlife

Life on
the edge.

Endangered

Monarch butterfly

The migratory monarch travels thousands of kilometres but has lost much of its milkweed habitat.

Vulnerable

Polar bear

Depends on Arctic sea ice, which is shrinking fast; about 26,000 remain.

Critically endangered

California condor

Saved from a handful of birds to more than 500 through captive breeding.

04 — Challenges

What is
at stake.

01

Wildfires

Hotter, drier summers are fueling larger and more intense fires in forests and grasslands.

02

Arctic warming

The Arctic is heating several times faster than the global average, thawing permafrost and ice.

03

Prairie & habitat loss

Most native grasslands have been converted to farms and cities.
